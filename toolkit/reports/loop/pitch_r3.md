- It does NOT replace your judgement. In our own simulations, a counselor who knows the student beats any model that

- It screens your whole caseload in one step (one CSV: student ID, scores, your rating, schools). It flags lists where

  can be off too (it over-warned about 2x in our hardest test), so risk is always shown as a range.
